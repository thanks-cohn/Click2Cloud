### Privacy and security boundaries

- Preserve the rule: **providers receive file bytes plus the required filename/metadata, never source/page URLs or browsing provenance**; keep URLs only in local History under existing privacy controls.

- Keep source URL validation, response MIME/body guard, private-address restrictions, bounded memory, aborts and provider-confirmed writes. Do not turn HTML or an inaccessible endpoint into a fake saved file.
