3. With existing authorization and an explicitly permitted disposable source/test prefix, right-click a small approved public test file. 4. After each single click, inspect `await chrome.storage.local.get('lastDepthClick')` and open Save Results. Record click UUID, safe code, item/parent/generation and related remote operation. A depth-click entry is NOT a transfer receipt. Check the separate remote receipt and independently inspect the exact R2 key/size; root must not become bucket/bucket/. Never blindly retry an uncertain upload.
5. Repeat with Save Results open/closed, refresh/reapply, extension reload/worker restart and twenty existing siblings where available. Confirm working non-Depth actions still behave as before, without editing their settings. Do not simulate permission failures by revoking owner grants or altering production Workers.

Notification/Shelf opening depends on Chrome policy/gesture; verify physically, do not infer success from a mock callback.
