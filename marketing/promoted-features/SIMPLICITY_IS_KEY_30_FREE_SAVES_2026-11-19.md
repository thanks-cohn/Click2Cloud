# Click2Cloud promotional concept — Simplicity Is Key

**Recorded:** November 19, 2026  
**Product:** Click2Cloud (Tinker & Co.)  
**Status:** Proposed marketing positioning and possible free-tier policy, **not evidence that the offer is implemented, released or live**.

## Primary message

# Simplicity Is Key.
## Just Add to Chrome. Log In, and Click Away!

**Easy to start. Powerful when you're ready.**

Right-click an eligible image, link or file and save it directly to your connected cloud. The idea is to minimize onboarding and avoid presenting API credentials, command lines and technical configuration to ordinary users where the supported OAuth workflow can do the work.

**Three-step consumer pitch:**
1. **Add to Chrome.** Install the extension.
2. **Log In.** Connect an authorized Cloudflare account and complete any required bucket/destination setup as simply as possible.
3. **Click Away!** Right-click eligible files and save to the chosen cloud.

**Suggested shorter hero line:** “Add to Chrome. Log In. Click Away!”

### End-card and short ad copy

> **CLICK2CLOUD**  
> **Simplicity Is Key.**  
> Just Add to Chrome. Log In, and Click Away!  
> *Cloud saving without the technical headache.*

**Suggested short website description:**

> Save from your browser straight to your cloud. Start with a simple connection and a right-click. Explore optional advanced features only when you need them.

**15-second ad concept:**
- Show a cluttered technical setup form in silhouette; cut to the clean Click2Cloud Remotes login.
- Text: **“Just Add to Chrome.”**
- User completes the actual supported cloud authorization/setup; text: **“Log In.”**
- User right-clicks eligible media and chooses their configured cloud destination; text: **“Click Away!”**
- End card: **“Simplicity Is Key.”** Show the verified destination result only after observed confirmation.

### Product positioning — simple outside, scriptable inside

- **Beginner-facing:** basic login, a clear destination, one right-click save, straightforward feedback. Do not expose unnecessary Worker/technical preparation details on the Minimal Remotes surface.
- **Optional power-user controls:** many folders, bulk URL exports, metadata/tags, automatically routed downloads, screenshots to cloud and verifiable transfer summaries **only when the respective features really ship**.
- **Developer-facing:** powerful existing V3+1.1/File API and scriptable operations, repeatable tests, extensible diagnostics and precise machine-readable results. Sophistication belongs under the hood, not in mandatory onboarding.
- The differentiator is **confidence without complexity**: “easy to use” must be supported by real cold-boot, login, right-click and successful-save tests.

## Proposed free-tier strategy — 30 saves per month

**Potential plan (NOT live):** Offer **30 free remote saves per month**, a deliberately approachable free tier.

**Competitive comparison:** Briskkit's “Save Anywhere — Right-Click Save to Dropbox & Google Drive” Chrome Web Store listing was checked on November 19, 2026 and advertised **20 free saves per month**. A Click2Cloud offer of **30** would be **10 more saves (50% more)** compared with that specific advertised limit **at the time of verification**.

**Possible advertising, only after implementation and release:**
- **“30 Free Cloud Saves Every Month.”**
- **“10 More Free Saves Than Save Anywhere.”** Only with an up-to-date substantiated competitor comparison, source/date/conditions, and comparable definition of a “save”.
- **“Start free. Save with a right-click.”**

**Caution before publishing:**
- This is a product/pricing **proposal** only. Define what counts as one save (file, retries, bulk operations), reset timing, whether there is a signup requirement, exclusions, and any user privacy/rate-limit implications. Build and test the quota logic before making the claim publicly.
- Reconcile this limited tier with Click2Cloud's separately discussed **“UNLIMITED FREE REMOTE”** positioning. Do not advertise both a 30-save monthly cap and unlimited free saves unless they explicitly apply to distinct, plainly defined plans or operations.
- Cloudflare R2 and Worker costs, connected-account permissions, and any required bucket initialization must remain truthful. The three-step pitch is the **UX goal** and should be used as a guaranteed experience only for flows independently verified to meet it.

## Promotion priority and placement

Use **Simplicity Is Key** as the primary onboarding message for nontechnical users. Treat “We Got the Receipts,” Get URLs/bulk exports, auto-routing, and scriptable APIs as deeper reasons to stay once someone understands the basic save.
