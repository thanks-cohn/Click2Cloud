Preserve existing batch choice, source and destination freezing, and verified paid unlimited behavior.
Renew on each monthly anniversary of the first validated installation/enrollment anchor, not first of calendar month, not 30-day duration and not each reinstall. Confirm/display actual next timestamp; avoid local timezone/DST drift. For existing installations recover the known installation/enrollment anchor; where unavailable use first validated enrollment, never invent an earlier date. Validate time using available trustworthy evidence, preserve last validated period and prevent backward/duplicate or arbitrary clock-jump renewal. No carryover. If several months elapsed, grant only current period once, not accumulated allowances. Late settlements use original admission period; old uncertainty does not consume new period credits. Persist anchor locally and in every replica. Validate renewal on startup, worker wake, reconnect, UI opening and before new free admission; no continuous polling or requirement Chrome be running at renewal. ## 4. Enrollment, missing files, modified files
- Known valid local state normally suffices; when missing or invalid recover newest valid compatible replica, apply conservative current-period conflict policy above, and preserve original anchor. - Invalid/corrupt/rolled-back manifest: attempt recovery from valid local/other replica. If no valid current-period eligibility can be established but original anchor is trustworthy, restrict unverified free admissions until state recovers or next independently validated renewal. Distinguish confirmed exhaustion from unavailable/unverified state; allow retry and truthful recovery information, never treat a typo in decorative prose as evidence of cheating. Unknown/unavailable/corrupt is not conclusive absence. Do not demand access to unrelated clouds or other accounts. All-copy deletion plus uninstall can therefore reset allowance; this limitation is accepted for V1.

Do not grant a fresh period solely for changing formats.
