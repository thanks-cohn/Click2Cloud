## Final release acceptance
- Reliable right-click after login and lifecycle events; auth/reconnection and destination continuity.
- Automatic upload AND download recovery, durable receipts, no duplicate publications, no false success, safe local file publication and integrity.
- Four providers individually verified both directions with ordinary UI smoke and authorized independent Laboratory acceptance; download-only works without prior upload. Round-trip verifies final disk files and hierarchy.
- Free allowance continuity and paid activation/restore with actual approved integration, clear policy and truthful limits.
- Simple UI, permission/privacy/store compliance, installable build, regression evidence, and owner release approval.
- Report candidate status explicitly: READY only if every required gate passes and external launch prerequisites are met; otherwise BLOCKED with smallest remaining work and exact reproducible evidence. Three 45-minute runs are an aggressive engineering allocation, not permission to conceal deficiencies or guarantee external approvals. No automatic public release.
